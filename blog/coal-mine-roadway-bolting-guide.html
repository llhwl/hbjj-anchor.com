Coal Mine Roadway Bolting: A Complete Guide with Resin Capsules

By Huaibei Jinjiu New Materials · Mining Support · 5 min read

Roadway bolting is the backbone of modern coal mine ground control. Get it right and you move crews safely and fast; get it wrong and you inherit spalling, roof falls, and downtime. This guide walks through how resin anchor capsules fit into a proper bolting cycle — and how to select the right capsule for your seam.

Why Roadway Bolting Matters in Coal Mines

Unlike surface construction, an underground roadway is surrounded by stressed, fractured rock that wants to move. Mechanical bolts alone may not fully bond to weak or laminated roof. A resin-grouted bolt fills the annulus and creates continuous contact, dramatically improving load transfer and reducing elongation.

The Role of Resin Capsules in Rock Bolting

A resin capsule turns a passive bolt into a fully bonded tendon:

  • It eliminates the air gap between bolt and borehole.
  • It reaches working strength in seconds to minutes (vs. hours for cement).
  • It performs in damp boreholes where cement struggles.

For high-gas or soft-roof coal seams, this speed and reliability is the difference between a safe cycle and a standing panel.

Step-by-Step Installation

  1. Mark & drill to design length (300–1800 mm common) and diameter.
  2. Push the capsule to the hole bottom.
  3. Insert the bolt and spin at correct RPM to rupture and mix the capsule.
  4. Hold until gel, then torque to plate.
  5. Verify with a torque or pull test per your support plan.
